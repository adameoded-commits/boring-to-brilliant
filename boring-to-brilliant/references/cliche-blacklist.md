# The B2B Cliché Blacklist

These words and phrases appear on almost every B2B website, which means they carry
almost no information. They're not forbidden, but each one should earn its place by
being followed with something specific. Usually it's better to delete them and say
the specific thing directly.

The same list lives in `scripts/cliche_check.py`, so keep both in sync if you edit.

## Empty superlatives
cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, best-in-class, world-class, industry-leading,
leading provider, next-generation, revolutionary, game-changing, groundbreaking,
unparalleled, unmatched, second to none, premier

## Vague verbs
empower, enable, leverage, unlock, transform, revolutionize, streamline, optimize,
maximize, elevate, drive (value/growth), harness, unleash

## Filler nouns
solutions, synergy, ecosystem, paradigm, value proposition, best practices,
one-stop shop, end-to-end, holistic, robust, scalable, seamless, turnkey

## Hollow promises
innovative, high-quality, reliable, trusted partner, customer-centric, passionate,
committed to excellence, peace of mind, the future of, take it to the next level,
we're excited to announce, sustainable future (without a number)

## How to fix
| Cliché | Ask | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| "reliable" | Reliable how? Measured how? | "10-year warranty", "99.x% uptime last year" |
| "innovative" | What's new that nobody else has? | Name the thing |
| "seamless integration" | With what, in how long? | "Connects to SAP in an afternoon" |
| "empower your team" | To do what they couldn't before? | The action itself |
| "sustainable future" | How much, by when? | "X tons of CO₂ a year at one site" |
